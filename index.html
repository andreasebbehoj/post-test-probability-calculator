Interpretation

Sensitivity and specificity describe how a diagnostic test performs among people with and without the disease. They do not, by themselves, tell us the probability that a patient has the disease after a test result. This post-test probability also depends on the estimated pre-test probability.

Pre-test probability is inherently uncertain. It may be informed by clinical characteristics, clinical experience, disease prevalence in comparable populations, or validated prediction models. When appropriate, a plausible range may better reflect this uncertainty than a single value.

This calculator is intended for educational and scientific purposes and does not provide medical advice. Results depend on the validity and applicability of the entered pre-test probability, sensitivity and specificity.

All calculations are performed locally in your browser. No entered information is transmitted or stored.
