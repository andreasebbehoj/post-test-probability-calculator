Interpretation

Sensitivity and specificity describe characteristics of a diagnostic test, but they do not by themselves determine the probability that an individual patient has the disease after testing. The post-test probability also depends on the estimated pre-test probability of disease.

Pre-test probability is often uncertain and should be interpreted as an estimate rather than an exact property of an individual patient.

This calculator is intended for educational and scientific purposes. It does not provide medical advice and does not determine whether a diagnostic test or treatment is appropriate for an individual patient. The validity of the result depends on the validity and applicability of the entered pre-test probability, sensitivity and specificity.
